# GPU Gaming Benchmark Data — Source Investigation & Verdict

Special-priority item from the task brief. **Goal:** can SPECTRA obtain a *reliable*,
*standardized* GPU gaming-benchmark dataset that is *legally usable* (no scraping where
terms prohibit it; no reproducing content whose terms forbid it)?

**Verdict up front: No source was found that is clearly usable as-is for bulk ingestion,
and none grants explicit free republication rights.** Details and one clean avenue are
below. Consequently **batch 001 imports no gaming-benchmark data.**

Method note: everything below was verified by fetching the listed pages on 2026-10-08
(by the research agent; page fetches that failed are listed explicitly — nothing was
assumed). Quotes are verbatim from fetched pages.

---

## 1. UL Solutions / 3DMark — NOT USABLE (bulk); permission-gated otherwise

Fetched: `https://www.ul.com/customer-resources/online-policies/terms-of-use` (the real
Terms URL; `/terms` and `/3dmark.com/terms` 404), `https://benchmarks.ul.com/eula`,
`https://www.3dmark.com/search`, `https://benchmarks.ul.com/services/retail-solutions`.

> "The Site is provided for your personal information and non-commercial use. The
> commercial use, reproduction, transmission or distribution of any information, software
> or other material made available through the Site without the prior written consent of
> UL Solutions is strictly prohibited. … You specifically agree not to access (or attempt
> to access), or systematically retrieve data from, any part of the Site through any
> automated means (including use of scripts, bots or web crawlers)."
> — UL Solutions Terms of Use §2

> "…the ownership rights to the Software as well as to the results generated by the
> Downloadable Software and sent to the Web Service shall solely remain with Futuremark
> and/or its licensors." — 3DMark EULA §2.2

> "disclose and publish … test results generated by the Downloadable Software, provided
> that the Instructions are followed and that the Licensee has obtained prior, written
> permission from Futuremark." — EULA §3.1.3/§4.1.3

> "You are not allowed to establish an automated performance estimation system with the
> help of performance information obtained if solely licensing the Downloadable Software."
> — EULA §5.4

- **No public results API.** UL sells this data: *"our dedicated laboratories have
  assessed more than 50 of the world's newest, most popular games… Our benchmark data
  comes in tailored packages"* — a commercial, retail-customer route.
- A few hand-transcribed Time Spy numbers for attribution would be a **legal gray zone**,
  not a verified green light; a writer's permission is the clean path.

## 2. TechPowerUp — NOT USABLE (bulk/AI); permission obtainable for a small set

Fetched: `https://www.techpowerup.com/robots.txt`, `/contact/`, `/privacy/`, and an
example standardized multi-GPU FPS review:
`https://www.techpowerup.com/review/gears-of-war-e-day-performance-benchmark/`
("35 GPUs Tested"), confirming the exact data shape SPECTRA wants (same game/settings
across many GPUs).

> "TechPowerUp content is made available for your personal, non-commercial use … Use of
> any device, tool, or process designed to data mine or scrape the content using automated
> means is prohibited without prior written permission from TechPowerUp. Prohibited uses
> include but are not limited to: … (3) creating or providing archived or cached data sets
> containing our content to others … Contact w1zzard@techpowerup.com for assistance."
> — TechPowerUp robots.txt

Copyright © 2004-2026 www.techpowerup.com. All rights reserved.

- Bulk ingestion = prohibited; dataset creation is called out explicitly.
- **Ask first** is the pragmatic path: `w1zzard@techpowerup.com` is publicly offered for
  exactly this kind of request. A non-commercial, attributed small set (e.g. ~2–3 review
  pages covering ~50 GPUs in one or two games) is the most promising legal source.

## 3. OpenBenchmarking.org / Phoronix Test Suite — UNKNOWN (blocked)

- `openbenchmarking.org` homepage, `www`, a test-profile page, and `/api` all returned
  **HTTP 403** on every attempt, so its result-data license **could not be verified** and
  gaming-profile licenses could not be read. Treat as UNKNOWN until browsed manually.
- The **software** is GPL-3.0 (verified on GitHub): PTS can run scripted, standardized
  tests; combined with the self-run route (§5) it is SPECTRA's best *tool*, but OB's
  hosted results remain unverified.
- `https://www.phoronix.com/legal`: *"Articles may not be copied or distributed without
  the prior written consent of Phoronix Media."* (covers articles, not OB result data.)

## 4. Tom's Hardware / Future plc — NOT USABLE (even manually)

> "You may not do any of the following, unless laws prohibit these restrictions or you
> have our advance written permission to do so: a. share, download, modify, copy,
> distribute, transmit, display, perform, reproduce, duplicate, publish, license, create
> derivative works from, or offer for sale any information contained on, or obtained from
> or through, the Services; … k. use any robot, mods, hacks, spider, crawlers or other
> automatic device, process, software or queries that intercepts, 'mines,' scrapes or
> otherwise accesses the Services … or engage in any manual process to do the same …"

Note clause (k) explicitly covers **manual** extraction too. Do not use Tom's Hardware
benchmarks.

## 5. Recommended path for SPECTRA

1. **Self-run benchmarks** (fully clean, zero third-party terms, full standardization and
   provenance): PTS (GPL-3.0) automates this; realistic on the hardware SPECTRA physically
   has. Best for a small, high-integrity anchor set.
2. **Ask TechPowerUp** for permission to reuse a small number of standardized multi-GPU
   FPS tables with attribution (non-commercial). Highest value-per-effort if granted.
3. If a permissioned source supplies data later, the **frontend must first be extended** —
   today `frontend/src/performance.js:24-40,94-96` hard-filters to
   `Geekbench 7` / `points` / `test_type ∈ {single-core, multi-core}`, and the GPU
   comparison benchmark panel is a hardcoded empty state (`App.jsx:3735-3754`). Rows
   added to `benchmark_results` with e.g. `benchmark_name='TechPowerUp'`, `test_type=
   'gaming-1080p'` would be stored by the API but **silently invisible** in the UI.

Sources consulted and their live verdicts: UL/3DMark (not usable / permission-gated),
TechPowerUp (not usable bulk / permission possible), OpenBenchmarking (unverified, 403),
Future/Tom's (not usable), GamersNexus (no permission granted; all-rights-reserved),
Notebookcheck (not assessed — no terms page fetched).

**Net recommendation: do not force gaming-benchmark ingestion now. Re-check
OpenBenchmarking from a real browser and, in parallel, ask TechPowerUp for a small
attributed set; treat the frontend filter as a prerequisite code ticket.**
