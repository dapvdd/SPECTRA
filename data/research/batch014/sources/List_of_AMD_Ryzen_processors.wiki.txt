{{Short description|none}}
[[Microprocessors]] of the [[Ryzen]] brand are a line of [[x86-64]] microprocessors manufactured by [[AMD]], based on the [[Zen microarchitecture]]. Ryzen microprocessors include: Ryzen 3, Ryzen 5, Ryzen 7, Ryzen 9, and [[Ryzen Threadripper]] (with up to 96 cores). All consumer desktop Ryzens (except PRO models), as well as all mobile processors with the HX suffix, have an unlocked [[CPU multiplier|multiplier]]. In addition, all Ryzen microprocessors support [[Simultaneous Multithreading]] (SMT), except for earlier Zen/Zen+ based desktop and mobile Ryzen 3 processors, and some models of the Zen 2 mobile Ryzen microprocessor.

=== Ryzen 9000 series ===
==== Fire Range (Zen 5/RDNA2 based)<span class="anchor" id="Fire Range"></span> ====
Common features of Ryzen 9000 Fire Range series:

* Socket: FL1
* All Models support DDR5-5600 in 128-bit mode; Maximum memory: 96 GB
* All models support 28 PCIe 5.0 lanes
* Native USB 10Gbps (USB 3.2 Gen 2 operation mode) : 4
* Native USB 2.0 (480 Mbit/s): 1
* iGPU: AMD Radeon 610M (2 CU @ 2200&nbsp;MHz)
* No NPU
* L1 cache: 80 KB (48 KB data + 32 KB instruction) per core
* L2 cache: 1 MB per core
* Fabrication process: TSMC N4 FinFET (CCD) + TSMC N6 FinFET (I/OD)

=== P100 series ===
==== (Ryzen AI Embedded P100 series, Zen 5/RDNA 3.5 based)<span class="anchor" id="RyzenP100 embedded"></span> ====

Model numbers with the "i" suffix are marketed for industrial use and support operating at colder ambient temperatures.<ref name="p100-series-pr"/> Model numbers with the "a" suffix are marketed for automotive use and are [[Automotive Electronics Council|AEC]]-Q100 qualified for reliability and longevity.<ref name="p100-series-pr"/>

Common features of Ryzen AI Embedded P100 series CPUs:
* Socket: [[Ball grid array|BGA]], FP8 package type.
* [[CPU cache|L3 cache]] (total): 8&nbsp;MB.
* All models support [[Multi-channel memory architecture#Dual-channel_architecture|dual-channel]] [[ECC memory|ECC]] [[DRAM]].
* All models support 14 [[PCIe 4.0]] lanes.
* Native [[10GbE]] ports (with [[Time-Sensitive Networking|TSN]]): 2<ref name="p100-series-pr">{{cite press release |date=January 5, 2026 |title=AMD Introduces Ryzen AI Embedded Processor Portfolio, Powering AI-Driven Immersive Experiences in Automotive, Industrial and Physical AI |url=https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-1-5-amd-introduces-ryzen-ai-embedded-processor-portfol.html |location=[[Santa Clara, California]] |publisher=[[AMD]] |agency=Globe Newswire |access-date=2026-01-13 }}</ref>
* Native USB 5Gbps (USB 3.2 Gen 1 operation mode): 1
* Native USB 5Gbps (USB 3.1 Gen 1 operation mode): 1<!--this cant be right. ref "p100-series-pr" says 3.1 and 3.2 but that means exactly the same thing-->
* Native USB 2.0 (480 Mbit/s): 4
* iGPU uses the [[RDNA 3.5]] microarchitecture and supports output at up to 120&nbsp;[[Frame rate|FPS]] to up to either 4 × [[4K resolution|4K]] or 2 × [[8K resolution|8K]] displays.<ref name="p100-series-pr"/>
* iGPU supports encoding/decoding video at up to 4K & 60&nbsp;Hz.
* All iGPUs support [[HDMI]] 2.1 and [[DisplayPort]] 2.0 output.
* [[Neural processing unit|NPU]] uses the [[XDNA 2]] architecture.<ref name="p100-series-pr"/>
* Rated longevity: "2.5 years (Standard), Up to 10 years (Extended)"<ref name="p100-series-pr"/>
