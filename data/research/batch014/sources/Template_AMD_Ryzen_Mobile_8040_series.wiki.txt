Key features of Ryzen 8040 notebook APUs:
* Socket: [[Ball_grid_array|BGA]] (FP7, FP7r2 or FP8 type packages).
* All models support [[DDR5 SDRAM|DDR5]]-5600 or [[LPDDR#LPDDR5X|LPDDR5X]]-7500 in [[Multi-channel memory architecture#Dual-channel architecture|dual-channel]] mode.
* CPU uses [[Zen_4|Zen4]] cores (Phoenix) or a combination of Zen4 and Zen4c cores (Phoenix2).
* GPU uses the [[RDNA 3]] (Navi 3) architecture.
* Some models include first-generation Ryzen AI [[Neural processing unit|NPU]] (XDNA) capable of 16 {{abbr|TOPS|Trillions of operations per second}}.
* All models support [[AVX-512]] using a half-width 256-bit FPU.
* [[PCI Express#PCI Express 4.0|PCIe 4.0]] support.
* Native USB 40Gbps (USB4) Ports: 2
* Native USB 10Gbps (USB 3.2 Gen 2 operation mode) Ports: 2
* Fabrication process: [[TSMC]] [[5 nm process|N4]] FinFET.
